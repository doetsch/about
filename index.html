Hermes Personal Sync

A private, single-user tool that lets the operator's own home automation and reporting software read their Google account on their own local network.

It is not a public service. It has exactly one user: the person named in the contact address below. It exists so that their health, calendar, mail (read-only), drive and document data can be fetched over HTTPS by their own machines and shown in a dashboard that never leaves their home network.

What it does:

  • Reads the operator's Google Health data (activity, sleep, body measurements) to build a daily personal health dashboard.
  • Reads Gmail and Contacts (read-only) and reads/writes Calendar, Drive, Docs and Sheets, to automate the operator's own notes, reports and schedule.
  • Runs entirely on hardware the operator owns, in their home.
